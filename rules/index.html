Play Yellow Car Rules

There aren’t many rules.
That’s kind of the point.

Spot a yellow car.

See it first and call “YELLOW CAR!”

First call gets the point.

If two people call it at exactly the same time, argue about it briefly, then award the point to whoever is most convincing. 😆

No double-counting.

The same yellow car only scores once.

The driver is exempt from physical celebrations while the car is moving.

Safety first. No tickling, knee squeezing or surprise attacks on the driver.

The winner earns bragging rights.

Optional prize: the winner gets to tickle the other players — but never the driver while the car is moving.

The gentle knee squeeze is fair game.

Surprisingly effective. Use responsibly.

No dangerous spotting.

Nobody unclips seatbelts, climbs around the car, hangs out a window or distracts the driver for a point.

Arguments are part of the game.

“I saw it first!” is acceptable. Full-scale family court proceedings are not required.

Yellow means yellow.

Gold, mustard, lime and suspicious beige may be challenged by the other players.

Bonus rules are encouraged.

Yellow trucks worth two points? Yellow convertibles worth three? A yellow Lamborghini wins the whole trip? Your car, your rules.

Actually… forget our rules if you want.

Make up your own. The only real rule is: have fun, keep the driver safe, and keep looking out the window.

Official Yellow Car ruling: the loudest person is not automatically right. 😄
