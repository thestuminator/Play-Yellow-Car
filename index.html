What is Play Yellow Car?

Ridiculously simple. Deliberately different.

Look out the window. Spot a yellow car. Shout “YELLOW CAR!” Score a point.

At the start of a journey, add the players and choose an avatar. A passenger keeps score. Play Yellow Car records the journey and declares a winner — while the game itself happens outside the window.

Technology working backwards

How it started

Born on the road. Built on a rainy Sunday.

Play Yellow Car began quite by accident. Cairns couple Stuart Carr and Dr Sabine Mercer started playing a ridiculously simple game together on their drives: spot a yellow car first, call “Yellow Car!”, and score a point.

There was no business plan, no app and certainly no intention to create a product. It was simply something they loved playing together.

Then Thomas discovered it — and the idea suddenly looked much bigger.
